📡 How to Set Up Your Live Stream:

  1. Go to YouTube Studio → Click "Create" → "Go Live"
  2. Set up your stream with title, description, and settings
  3. Open OBS Studio and configure your scenes
  4. In YouTube Studio, copy your Stream key
  5. In OBS:
    • Settings → Stream
    • Service: YouTube
    • Paste your stream key
  6. Click "Start Streaming" in OBS
  7. Once live, copy your YouTube video URL
  8. Get the video ID from the URL:
    https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YOUR_VIDEO_ID
  9. Update this page: Replace the stream offline message with:
    <iframe src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/YOUR_VIDEO_ID" frameborder="0" allowfullscreen></iframe>
  10. Add live chat: Replace the chat offline message with:
    <iframe src="https://www.youtube.com/live_chat?v=YOUR_VIDEO_ID&embed_domain=localhost" frameborder="0"></iframe>
