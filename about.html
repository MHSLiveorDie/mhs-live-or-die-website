About The Channel

Where survival horror meets intense warfare

Welcome to MHS Live or Die

This channel is dedicated to bringing you the best in survival horror and tactical warfare gaming. Whether it's navigating the terrifying corridors of Silent Hill, surviving the onslaught in Resident Evil, or dominating the battlefield in all-out warfare, this is where gaming intensity meets skill.

I focus on creating content that challenges both myself and my viewers - from no-damage runs and professional difficulty playthroughs to epic battlefield moments and tactical gameplay.

What You'll Find Here:

  • Survival Horror Challenges - Pushing games to their limits with unique challenge runs
  • In-Depth Guides - Comprehensive walkthroughs and tips for beating the toughest games
  • Battlefield Domination - Epic gameplay, weapon guides, and tactical strategies
  • Easter Eggs & Secrets - Hidden details and secrets you might have missed
  • Boss Fight Breakdowns - Strategies for defeating the hardest bosses
